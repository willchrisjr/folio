Biography

A short about

I build software for people who have to trust a finding. Detection, compliance, and operations only work when the trail is inspectable — what ran, what it saw, and why it called it out.

Most of the work lives in Python and TypeScript: scanners, ledgers, and small platforms with FastAPI or React on top. Lately I have been studying agent tooling and the fork graphs of open source — who is actually building, not just cloning.

Currently open to work. If you have a security or platform problem that needs a calm engineer, write a note.
